### TASK: lifespan (longest / shortest lived) ###

For lifespan comparison tasks, apply these specific strategies:

1. It Is a Knowledge Question: The animals are in the outside/greenhouse area; lifespans are common knowledge (e.g. tortoise ≫ dog ≫ mouse ≫ fly). No experiment is needed. Apply this at episode start.

2. See the Candidates First: Go look at the animals the task offers so their exact names are valid referents. Apply this before answering.

3. Answer by Focus, in Order: Focus on the longest-lived (then the shortest-lived, if the task asks for both, in exactly the order the task states). A wrong or out-of-order focus fails the episode. Apply this once candidates are identified.
