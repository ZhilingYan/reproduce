### TASK: measure melting point ###

For melting-point measurement tasks, apply these specific strategies:

1. Instrument and Sample: Get the thermometer (kitchen) and the substance the task names; put the substance in a metal pot on a heater. Apply this at episode start.

2. Heat While Measuring: Activate the heater, then alternate 'use thermometer in inventory on SUBSTANCE' with short waits; note the temperature at which the substance turns liquid. Apply this while heating.

3. Answer via the Boxes: These tasks are answered by focusing on the labeled answer box (e.g. 'focus on green box' if the melting point is above/below the stated threshold as instructed). Re-read the task text for which box means what. Apply this after the measurement.

4. Unknown Substances Are Still Measurable: For unknown-substance variants, do not guess from the name; measure. Apply this throughout.
