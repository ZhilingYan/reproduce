### TASK: mendelian genetics ###

For genetics tasks, apply these specific strategies:

1. Read the Trait in Question: The task asks whether a trait (e.g. white flower color) is dominant or recessive in a (possibly unknown) plant. Apply this at episode start.

2. Cross and Count: Self- or cross-pollinate the given plants (greenhouse tools: pollination via bees or manual), grow offspring, and count phenotypes across enough offspring. A 3:1 ratio marks the majority trait dominant. Apply this through the experiment.

3. Be Patient: Growth needs repeated waiting and watering, like grow tasks. Apply this between generations.

4. Answer via the Boxes: Focus on the answer box (dominant/recessive) per task text. Apply this after counting.
