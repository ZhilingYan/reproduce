### TASK: change of state (boil / melt / freeze) ###

For state-of-matter tasks, apply these specific strategies:

1. Locate the Substance First: Find the exact substance named in the task (search kitchen, workshop, foundry) and focus on it before any heating or cooling. The focus-on-substance step is a scored subgoal and must come first. Apply this at episode start.

2. Container Before Heater: Move the substance into a suitable container (metal pot for heating) and keep it alone in that container before placing it on a device. Apply this once the substance is in hand.

3. Pick the Right Device: Stove and oven reach moderate temperatures; the blast furnace in the foundry is needed for metals with high melting points (e.g. lead). Freezing uses the freezer or the outside cold. Activate the device after the container is on it. Apply this when choosing where to heat or cool.

4. Wait and Measure, Do Not Assume: State changes take time. Alternate 'use thermometer ... on ...' / 'examine ...' with 'wait1' until the observation names the new state (liquid, gas, solid). Apply this after the device is running.

5. Never Focus on the Device: Focus only on the substance the task names. Focusing on the stove, oven, or container ends the episode with failure. Apply this at every focus decision.
