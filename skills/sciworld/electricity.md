### TASK: power a component ###

For power-component tasks, apply these specific strategies:

1. Identify Source and Target: The task names a component (e.g. light bulb, motor) and may restrict the power source (renewable = solar panel / wind turbine; nonrenewable = battery / generator). Re-read the task text before wiring. Apply this at episode start.

2. Wire a Complete Loop: Connect source terminal 1 to component terminal 1 and component terminal 2 back to source terminal 2 with wires; a single wire is not a circuit. Apply this with parts collected in the workshop or outside.

3. Activate the Source: Solar panels need... nothing but daylight; generators and turbines may need activation. Activate after wiring. Apply this once the loop is closed.

4. Focus per Task Text: Usually focus on the powered component when it is on. Apply this at the end.
