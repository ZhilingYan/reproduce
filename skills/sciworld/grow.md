### TASK: grow (plant / fruit) ###

For grow tasks, apply these specific strategies:

1. Seeds First: Find the seed (seed jar) the task names — usually in the greenhouse or kitchen. Focus on the seed as soon as it is identified; that focus is the first scored subgoal. Apply this at episode start.

2. Plant in Soil: Move the seed into a flower pot with soil (greenhouse) or outside ground. A cup or a bare table will not grow anything. Apply this after the focus-on-seed step.

3. Water and Wait: Pour water on the soil (jug/sink), then wait repeatedly. Growth passes through life stages (seed, seedling, adult, reproducing); re-examine the plant between waits. Apply this once planted.

4. Fruit Needs Pollination: For grow-fruit, after flowers appear, pollination is needed (bees in the greenhouse do it if flowers are near the hive); keep waiting and observing until fruit forms. Apply this in the flowering stage.

5. Never Focus on Pot or Room: Focus only on the seed/plant/fruit the task names. Apply this at every focus decision.
