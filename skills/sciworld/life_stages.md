### TASK: identify life stages ###

For life-stage tasks, apply these specific strategies (this family is sudden-death: any wrong or out-of-order focus fails immediately):

1. Read the Required Order: The task asks to focus on the stages of a named animal/plant in life-cycle order (egg/seed -> young -> adult). List the stages mentally before touching anything. Apply this at episode start.

2. Find All Stages First: Locate every stage instance in the environment (outside, greenhouse) before the first focus; focusing early then searching risks an out-of-order mistake. Apply this before any focus.

3. Focus Strictly in Order: Focus on stage 1, then stage 2, then stage 3, with nothing in between. Do not focus on anything else, ever. Apply this once all stages are located.
