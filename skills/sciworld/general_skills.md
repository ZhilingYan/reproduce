### GENERAL SKILLS ###

Here are some useful general strategies for completing science tasks in this environment:

1. The admissible actions list shows every action that can be executed right now - choose from it.

2. Focus is a commitment: "focus on OBJ" only on the object(s) the task asks for; focusing on anything else immediately ends the episode with failure.

3. Some outcomes need time (heating, growing): repeat measurements or wait instead of assuming failure.

4. Always verify what is present before claiming something is impossible; check the room and containers to confirm where task-relevant objects are.

5. If the target object is not in this room, move to other rooms to find it.
