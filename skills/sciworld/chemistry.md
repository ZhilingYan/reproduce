### TASK: chemistry (mix / paint colors) ###

For mixing tasks, apply these specific strategies:

1. Collect Ingredients Exactly: Find every ingredient the task names (read the task text carefully) before mixing anything. Chemistry supplies are in the workshop or kitchen; paints are in the art studio. Apply this at episode start.

2. One Container: Pour or move all ingredients into a single container (jug, cup, bowl); the have-substances-alone-in-one-container subgoal is scored. Apply this once all ingredients are found.

3. Mix the Container: Use 'mix CONTAINER' on the container holding the ingredients, not on the ingredients themselves. Apply this after everything is inside.

4. Paint Color Rules: Secondary colors come from two primaries (red+blue=violet, blue+yellow=green, red+yellow=orange); tertiary colors mix a primary with a neighboring secondary. Mix in a cup or on the palette in the art studio. Apply this to chemistry-mix-paint tasks.

5. Focus on the Product: When the task asks to focus, focus on the resulting mixture/paint the task names — never on the raw paints or the container before mixing. Apply this after the mix succeeds.
